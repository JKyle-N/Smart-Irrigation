  new-Nano-only feature as I nearly assumed before checking).

Does NEW ESP2 require NEW ESP1?  NO.
  Every ESP2 change this pass series is strictly "more text appended after an existing comma/pipe" in
  a reply ESP1 already knew how to receive (FLOW_FAIL's pulse count, PWR_FAIL/SAFE_STOP/DOSE_TIMEOUT/
  PH_FAIL/EC_FAIL/SENSOR_FAIL's measured-value detail). OLD ESP1's identical generic-passthrough parsing
  (same evidence as above) handles a longer string exactly as well as a short one.

Does NEW Nano require NEW ESP1/ESP2?  NO.

  Nano behavior because there isn't any.

Nano can stay on its current (OLD) firmware indefinitely with zero functional loss and zero risk to
ESP1/ESP2, new or old.
